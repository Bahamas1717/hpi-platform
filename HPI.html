Procurement Transformation Assessment
Six Pillar Maturity Diagnostic

Rate your procurement function across six critical pillars on a 1-5 scale. Each pillar progresses from reactive, tactical operations (Level 1) through to autonomous, AI-augmented strategic value creation (Level 5). Your scores generate a maturity profile, gap analysis, and specific recommendations to close each gap.

How to Score
1 = Reactive: No structured capability, manual and ad hoc processes.   2 = Emerging: Basic tools in place, some visibility, mostly reactive.   3 = Established: Defined processes, regular cadence, some proactive management.   4 = Advanced: Predictive analytics, integrated systems, data-driven decisions.   5 = Autonomous: AI-augmented, self-optimising, continuous improvement embedded.
Sustainable Value Creation
People, Planet, Profit Framework

Rate your procurement function's contribution across three stakeholder dimensions. Each criterion is scored 1-5, from no structured capability to industry-leading practice. Your scores generate a Stakeholder Value Profile with specific recommendations to improve each area.

Why This Matters for Philips
Philips's purpose is to improve people's health and well-being through meaningful innovation, aiming to improve 2.5 billion lives per year by 2030, including 400 million in underserved communities. That purpose is operational, not aspirational. Procurement decisions that optimise only for cost miss the broader value equation. This framework ensures supplier and initiative evaluations account for workforce impact (People), environmental sustainability (Planet), and financial returns (Profit), creating a triple-bottom-line lens aligned with Philips's 2030 Impact Ambitions and ESG commitments.
